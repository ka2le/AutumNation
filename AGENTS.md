# Balance work

For economy, progression, goals, simulated players, or balance tests, follow the
standing testing standard in [BALANCE.md](BALANCE.md) and the runner instructions
in [tools/balance/README.md](tools/balance/README.md).

Read the [common testing blind spots](BALANCE.md#common-testing-blind-spots--read-before-drawing-balance-conclusions)
before drawing conclusions. A little-of-everything or buy-what-is-available player is only a reference;
test deliberate committed builds, milestone-stop/delayed builds, and concentrated versus mixed staffing.
Several strong paths with different commitments are desirable; equal income shares are not the target.

Use the current completion-focused player as the main benchmark. Keep strong
specialization and source-skipping challengers: do not force the tester to buy a
desired mix. The target is useful mixed development across phases, with no source
made irrelevant and no consistently dominant shortcut that ignores the others.
Distinguish paid progression from fully upgraded capacity fixtures. Historical
balance concerns must be rechecked against current game code and capable players.

Honey's intended identity is a commitment choice: minimal investment or a fully
developed, multiplying engine can both be valid. Leaves provide the starting
economy and a lasting foundation. Do not cheapen honey merely because partial
development loses; compare a properly staffed, fully developed honey strategy.
